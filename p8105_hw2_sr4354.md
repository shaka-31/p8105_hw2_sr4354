The NYC Transit dataset contains information on subway station entrances
and exits, including the subway line, station name and location, routes
served, entrance type, entry access, vending availability, and ADA
accessibility. The original dataset contained 1,868 observations and 32
variables. I retained the variables relevant to the questions of
interest and converted the Entry variable from a character variable
indicating “YES” or “NO” to a logical variable. The resulting dataset
contains 1,868 observations and 19 variables. Each observation
represents a station entrance or exit, with station-level information
repeated across entrances and exits. The data are not fully tidy because
routes served by a station are stored across multiple variables (Route1
through Route11) rather than in a single route variable, so the route
information will need to be reformatted for further analysis.
